Rustboro City
Kanazumi City カナズミシティ 		TrainersItems
North Exit: Route 115
South Exit: Route 104
East Exit: Route 116

Rustoboro City is the first big city in Hoenn. This city features a variety of homes and important buildings. Most notably is Devon Corporation's headquarters which is where you need to go to revive Fossils.

Trainer School
The city is also home to the Pokémon Trainer School of Hoenn where characters will teach the basics of battling.

Gym
The Rustboro City Gym is the first Gym in Hoenn and doesn't feature a massive puzzle, but is decked out as a museum. This gym has got a few trainers in who are bypassable through a path. At the end is the Rock-type Gym Leader, Roaxanne, who will give the Stone Badge when defeated as well as TM39, Rock Tomb.
